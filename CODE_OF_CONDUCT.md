# Code of Conduct

Tech4Life & Beyond builds impact technology with integrity.
Participation requires respectful, lawful, and safety-minded collaboration.

## Standards

We commit to:
- Respectful, professional interaction
- Clear, evidence-based disagreements
- Protection of confidential/legal-sensitive information
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, or doxxing

## Scope

Applies to issues, PRs, discussions, and any collaboration around TOIL.

## Reporting

Report misconduct privately to repository maintainers.
If the issue is security-related, follow `SECURITY.md`.
