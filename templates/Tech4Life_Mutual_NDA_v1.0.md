# Tech4Life Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) v1.0

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement")** is entered into by and between:

**Tech4Life & Beyond LLC**, a Florida Limited Liability Company ("Tech4Life"),

and

**[Counterparty Legal Name]** ("Receiving Party" / "Disclosing Party" as applicable).

Together referred to as the "Parties".

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to enable confidential discussions related to:
- Product concepts
- Prototypes and manufacturing discussions
- Licensing negotiations
- Technical, commercial, or strategic information

without risk of unauthorized disclosure or misuse.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:
- Product designs, diagrams, specifications
- Prototypes (physical or conceptual)
- Business models, pricing, and royalty terms
- Contributor, community, or governance information
- Any non-public information disclosed verbally, visually, or in writing

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:
- Is publicly available through no fault of the receiving party
- Is independently developed without reference to the disclosed information
- Is rightfully received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality

---

## 3. Obligations of the Parties

Each Party agrees to:
- Use Confidential Information solely for the stated purpose
- Not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without written consent
- Protect Confidential Information with reasonable care

---

## 4. Mutual Nature

This Agreement is **mutual**.

Both Parties may act as disclosing or receiving parties, and both are equally bound by the obligations herein.

---

## 5. Exclusions & Ethical Alignment

This Agreement does not:
- Override TOIL licensing terms
- Restrict ethical whistleblowing
- Prevent disclosure required by law or court order

Any required disclosure must provide prompt notice to the other Party where legally permitted.

---

## 6. Term

- This Agreement begins on the Effective Date
- Confidentiality obligations survive for **5 years** after termination

---

## 7. No Transfer of Rights

This Agreement does not:
- Grant any license
- Transfer ownership
- Create partnership, employment, or agency

All rights not expressly granted are reserved.

---

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Florida, USA**, without regard to conflict of law principles.

---

## 9. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes prior agreements on this subject.
