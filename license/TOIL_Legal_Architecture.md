# Tech4Life Operating System (TLOS)
## BLOCK 9 — TOIL Legal Architecture

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the **legal backbone** of the Tech4Life Open Impact License (TOIL) v1.0.

TOIL exists to:
- Protect creators
- Enable global collaboration
- Allow open non-commercial use
- Enforce fair commercial participation
- Sustain Tech4Life & Beyond as an entity

TOIL replaces traditional patent-first dependency with a **license-first operating model**.

---

## 2. Legal Philosophy

TOIL is based on three legal pillars:

1. **Copyright & Documentation** — authorship and timestamped publication
2. **Contract Law** — enforceable agreements for commercial use
3. **Trademark Usage** — protection through attribution and branding rules

Power comes from clarity, not secrecy.

---

## 3. Scope of TOIL

TOIL applies to:
- Physical products
- Hardware designs
- Software and firmware
- AI models and datasets
- Systems and methodologies
- Educational and operational frameworks

Every asset published by Tech4Life & Beyond is assumed TOIL-covered unless explicitly stated otherwise.

---

## 4. License Structure

### A. Non-Commercial Use

Permitted without payment:
- Personal use
- Educational use
- Research and experimentation

Requirements:
- Attribution to Tech4Life & Beyond
- No removal of branding
- No claim of exclusive ownership

---

### B. Commercial Use

Commercial use is **not automatic**.

Any entity wishing to:
- Manufacture
- Sell
- Distribute
- Monetize

Must sign a **TOIL Royalty Agreement**.

Terms are defined per product and per partner.

---

## 5. Royalty Logic

Royalty agreements define:
- Percentage of net sales or per-unit fee
- Payment frequency
- Reporting obligations

Default principles:
- Fairness over maximization
- Sustainability over short-term gain
- Alignment with product impact

---

## 6. Revenue Distribution

Collected royalties are distributed as follows (configurable per product):

- Product Cell contributors
- Tech4Life & Beyond operational fund
- Impact & reinvestment reserve

Exact percentages are defined in the Collaboration Agreement.

---

## 7. Enforcement Mechanisms

If TOIL is violated:

1. Written notice
2. Cease-and-desist request
3. License revocation
4. Legal action under applicable jurisdiction

TOIL enforcement relies on:
- Public documentation
- Timestamped GitHub records
- Clear attribution requirements

---

## 8. Jurisdiction & Governing Law

Primary governing law:
- State of Florida, USA

International enforcement relies on:
- Contract law
- Copyright treaties
- Trademark misuse claims

---

## 9. Relationship to Patents

TOIL does not prohibit patents.

However:
- Patents are optional
- Patents must not contradict TOIL openness
- TOIL publication creates prior art

Patents serve as **defensive tools**, not primary strategy.

---

## 10. IPO & Regulatory Readiness

TOIL is designed to be:
- Auditable
- Transparent
- Repeatable

This enables:
- Investor due diligence
- Regulatory compliance
- Public market readiness

TOIL assets are treated as **licensed IP portfolios**.

---

## 11. License Evolution

TOIL versions may evolve.

Rules:
- No retroactive removal of rights
- New versions apply forward only
- Changes require Architect consensus

---

## 12. Disclaimer

TOIL is provided in good faith.

Commercial partners are encouraged to seek independent legal counsel.

---

**TOIL protects openness by making responsibility enforceable.**
